Mentoring

Particle physics with LEGO

A one week holiday project organized, funded and located at the Katinka-Platzhoff Foundation in Hanau. The week featured a first introduction to particle physics for children from 6-14 using LEGO building blocks during the easter holidays. A visit to the GSI accelerator facility was the highlight on the last day.

2024 · Katinka-Platzhoff Foundation, Hanau
