Current research

FRISCO3 - A Multi-fluid hydrodynamics with continuous decoupling

  • The FRISCO3 model
    The ACRONYM (FRISCO3) is a relativistic multi-fluid hydrodynamic model with continuous hadron decoupling. The model disentangles the hydrodynamic equations of motion of the whole system into multiple fluids. A useful choice is to treat the two incoming nuclei as two distinct nuclear fluids, which generate the equilibrated fireball fluid dynamically via friction terms. The fluid-fluid friction terms are modeled using the inelastic nucleon-nucleon cross section for energy- and longitudinal momentum loss as well as baryon number transport into the fireball, i.e. stopping. The continuous decoupling generalizes the commonly applied Cooper-Frye procedure and overcomes its caveats along the way. The continuous decoupling determines the local decoupling rate of a hadron based on its origin cell's emissivity, which is given from the differential scattering rate, the in-medium width and the expansion rate, and the survival probability or optical depth along the hadron's trajectory to the free streaming regime.
